# Financial Services

The Financial Services domain covers regulated activities such as KYC, AML, onboarding, and fraud prevention across institutions and jurisdictions.

## Scope
Financial institutions operate under strict regulatory frameworks and must execute KYC, AML, and related controls across products, segments, and jurisdictions ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#The critical role of KYC|The critical role of KYC]]).

## Regulatory Context
Regulatory regimes cited include BSA, USA PATRIOT Act, EU AMLDs, MAS, and FATF guidance, reflecting multi-jurisdictional compliance demands ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#The critical role of KYC|The critical role of KYC]]).
