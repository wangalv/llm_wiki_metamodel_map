# Confidence Score

A numeric measure guiding automatic approval, additional verification, or escalation in KYC.

## Explanation
Supervisor-guided thresholds use high (>95%), medium (75–95%), and low (<75%) confidence to drive approvals, extra checks, or human review ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#Agentic AI Orchestration Layer|Agentic AI Orchestration Layer]]).
