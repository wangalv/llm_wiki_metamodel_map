# Legacy KYC Bottlenecks

Traditional KYC systems rely on batch and manual steps, blocking instant onboarding and real-time validation.

## Explanation
Legacy systems process in batches and use manual validation, preventing instant onboarding and consistent compliance across jurisdictions ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#Current challenges|Current challenges]]).
Without event-driven capabilities they cannot integrate modern AI/ML or adapt to new fraud patterns without reconfiguration ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#Current challenges|Current challenges]]).
