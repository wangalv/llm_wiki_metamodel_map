# Know Your Customer (KYC)

KYC is a core capability to verify identities, detect fraud, manage risk, and meet AML/CTF compliance in financial services.

## Explanation
KYC protects financial systems by verifying customer identities, preventing fraud, managing risk, and supporting AML/CTF compliance ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#The critical role of KYC|The critical role of KYC]]).
KYC complexity increases as institutions expand across products, segments, and jurisdictions with varied regulatory frameworks ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#The critical role of KYC|The critical role of KYC]]).

## Drivers for Modernization
Legacy systems, batch processing, and manual handoffs impede real-time validation and increase operational risk, driving modernization needs ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#Content|Content]]; [[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#Current challenges|Current challenges]]).
