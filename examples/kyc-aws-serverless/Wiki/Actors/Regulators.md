# Regulators

Regulators mandate KYC, AML, and CTF controls across jurisdictions and set frameworks institutions must follow.

## Explanation
Regulators worldwide require financial institutions to implement KYC processes and operate under frameworks like BSA, USA PATRIOT Act, EU AMLDs, MAS, and FATF ([[2026-09-28-modernizing-kyc-aws-serverless#The critical role of KYC|The critical role of KYC]]).
